Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 · Effective Date: October 28, 2026
Document data, account records, and service providers

1. Overview

Pressmark creates PDF documents and previews from the details you provide. This policy covers pressmark.dev, its API, and connections from assistants such as ChatGPT.

Pressmark processes document content for rendering and keeps separate account, billing, and usage records. When customer sync is enabled, it also records contact details and document summaries in our customer-management service and can start automated follow-up. Generating a PDF does not delete those records.

2. Information We Process

Pressmark processes the following information when you use the corresponding features:

  • Document Generation Inputs: Party names, corporate addresses, line item descriptions, rates, quantities, currency symbols, invoice identifiers, notes, and payment terms provided in generation requests.
  • Digital Signatures & Logos: Base64-encoded image assets submitted for inclusion in headers or signature blocks.
  • Account and session information: Your name, email, sign-in method, session records, and paid-access status. Guest access also creates a session.
  • Payment and checkout information: Stripe handles payment checkout. Pressmark records the selected plan, payment amount, payment-event identifiers, and access entitlement. Pressmark does not receive your full card number or bank credentials.
  • Usage records: Requested actions, document type, access channel, processing time, and outcome. These records help us operate and measure the service.

3. Storage and customer records

Document rendering and customer records have different storage behavior:

  • Documents and previews: Pressmark processes your supplied content to create the requested output. Temporary rendering assets may be cached. The Studio can also save documents and company details in your browser.
  • Temporary PDF downloads: PDF tools called through an assistant over HTTP can return a download link. Pressmark holds that PDF in memory for the download. The link expires after 15 minutes and stops working if the service restarts. Anyone with the link can download the PDF while it is valid. Expired PDFs are removed during cleanup, which runs every 30 seconds. These download copies are not written to the account database or a document archive. Download links may be unavailable when a PDF exceeds 8 MiB or temporary storage is full.
  • Account, billing, and usage records: These are stored separately from rendered documents. Expired sessions and older payment-event and usage records are removed periodically. Account entitlements and customer records are not deleted when rendering finishes.
  • Document-generation sync: When customer sync is enabled and an accepted email is available, Pressmark creates or updates a customer record. It uses the signed-in email when available; otherwise it uses the sender email, or the recipient email if the sender email is absent. This can happen without signing in.
  • Fields shared during generation: The selected email is recorded with the sender's name, phone number, company, and address, when supplied. The record also includes the document number, title, type, total, currency, and how it was created. Tags record watermark status, the presence of signatures, and the selected compliance setting. Document type and total can also be used to select follow-up. This sync does not send the PDF or line-item descriptions.
  • Account activity and follow-up: Email sign-in, checkout, payment, and refund events can also update customer records. Depending on the event, access status, document type, and total, the service can update a sales opportunity and enroll the contact in automated follow-up.
  • Model training: Pressmark does not use submitted document content to train AI models. An assistant you use to access Pressmark has its own data policies.

4. Third-Party Integrations

Pressmark uses these services for the corresponding functions:

  • OpenAI: Provides connected ChatGPT features and sign-in where available. Its privacy policy applies to data shared through those features.
  • Stripe: Payment processing for subscriptions and single-pass watermark removal. Governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • HighLevel: Stores the customer records described above and runs configured follow-up workflows in Pressmark's customer-management account.
  • Cloudflare: Website delivery and protection against abusive traffic.

5. Security Measures

Pressmark uses encrypted connections, request-size limits, rate limits, and checks on document inputs and asset paths.

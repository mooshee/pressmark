# Pressmark Document Archetypes Reference

Author: Daniel Hallman

This reference details the document archetypes supported by the Pressmark Vector Engine.

## Invoicing & Commercial Billing
- `invoice`: Standard commercial or corporate billing with itemized tables, payment terms, and bank/wire instructions.
- `invoice_freelance`: Designed for independent consultants, contractors, and agencies. Emphasizes billable hours, day rates, milestone breakdowns, and personal branding accents.
- `invoice_construction`: Progress billing compliant with AIA-style workflows. Features retainage calculations, work-in-place progress percentages, stored materials, and labor breakdowns.
- `invoice_medical`: CMS-compliant patient and insurance statements featuring CPT/HCPCS procedure codes, diagnosis codes (ICD-10), insurance copays, and deductible breakdowns.
- `invoice_saas`: Modern recurring subscription statement featuring tier breakdowns, per-seat licensing, API overages, and usage metrics.
- `invoice_minimal`: Clean Swiss modernist monochrome aesthetic with ultra-fine typographic rules and high information density.
- `proforma_invoice`: International trade and customs documentation detailing commercial goods valuation, harmonized tariff codes (HTS), origin country, and Incoterms.

## Sales, Proposals & Quotes
- `estimate`: Preliminary cost projection detailing projected labor and materials with explicit validity expiration dates (e.g. 30 days).
- `quotation_tender`: Formal procurement bid for enterprise or governmental RFP responses featuring project milestones, technical deliverables, and fixed-price bids.

## Fulfillment & Accounting
- `receipt`: Point-of-sale confirmation documenting settled payment, transaction reference IDs, and payment method details.
- `purchase_order`: Formal commercial procurement authorization issued by a buyer to a vendor detailing approved PO numbers and delivery instructions.
- `packing_slip`: Physical shipping manifest itemizing contents, package dimensions, weights, and handling instructions (omitting financial figures).
- `credit_note`: Formal negative adjustment or refund instrument referencing original invoice numbers and credit reason.

## Contracts & Real Estate
- `agreement` / `agreement_sow`: Bilateral binding agreements and Statements of Work detailing scope, milestones, and mutual signature blocks.
- `nda`: Mutual or unilateral non-disclosure agreement with confidentiality obligations and survival terms.
- `residential_lease`: Full residential lease agreement with statutory disclosures, security deposit terms, and tenant rules.
- `commercial_nnn_lease`: Triple-net commercial lease with Base Rent and CAM operating expense breakdowns.
- `rent_roll`: Multi-unit schedule with tenant names, unit numbers, square footage, monthly rent, and lease terms.
- `closing_settlement_statement`: Real estate escrow closing statement itemizing buyer/seller charges, credits, and cash to close.
